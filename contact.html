Inviting me to speak

I enjoy speaking about my work, and a little information upfront helps me plan and respond to invitations. If you'd like me to give a talk, workshop, or similar engagement, here is the information that is most helpful to provide:

  • The title, theme, dates, location (or virtual platform) of the event.
  • What prompted the invitation, and what aspects of my work you would like me to focus on.
  • Possible formats and duration of my contribution.
  • Whether the invitation is for a keynote and how many keynotes and non-keynote talks are planned.
  • The expected audience size and their background.
  • Whether any press or media will be in attendance.
  • Whether the event will be livestreamed, recorded, and how the recordings will be used or distributed.
  • The proposed compensation or honorarium. This guide from Speaker Agency UK may be helpful.

I do not ordinarily expect an honorarium for academic events and events run by non-profit organisations. For in-person engagements, I usually ask that travel and accommodation expenses are covered by the inviting organisation, including for academic and non-profit-run events.

Advisory boards and consultancy

I'm open to advisory and consultancy opportunities. I'm most likely to say yes when the role connects closely to my research: how AI affects the way people think and work, and designing AI as a tool for thought. I appreciate requests that are thoughtful about scope, commitments, and compensation.

Can you endorse me, or recommend someone...?

I often receive requests to endorse arXiv submissions or to suggest alternative contacts, especially when I am unable to accept a speaking, advisory, or consultancy engagement myself. I'm unfortunately not able to help with these requests from people I haven't worked with, as I want to ensure that any endorsement or introduction I make is meaningful and fair to those involved.

If we've already worked together, I'm happy for you to get in touch about these matters. Otherwise, I appreciate your understanding that I may not be able to assist.
