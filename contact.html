Inviting me to speak

I enjoy speaking about my work, and these details help me respond quickly. If inviting me to give a talk, workshop, or similar engagement, it really helps to provide the following upfront:

  • The title, theme, dates, location (or virtual platform) of the event.
  • Why you are inviting me specifically to this event, and what aspects of my work you would like me to focus on.
  • The expected format and duration of my contribution.
  • Whether the invitation is for a keynote and, if so, how many other keynotes and non-keynote talks are planned.
  • The expected audience size and their background.
  • Whether any press or media will be in attendance.
  • Whether the event will be livestreamed, recorded, and how the recordings will be used or distributed.
  • The proposed compensation or honorarium. This guide from Speaker Agency UK may be helpful.

I do not ordinarily expect an honorarium for academic events and events run by non-profit organisations. If I am required to travel to attend the event in person, I would ordinarily expect travel and accommodation expenses to be covered by the inviting organisation, including for academic and non-profit-run events.

Advisory board and consultancy

If you are inviting me to join an advisory board or to provide consultancy services, I would be grateful if you could include the following details in your initial request:

  • The nature and scope of the advisory or consultancy role.
  • The expected time commitment and duration of the engagement.
  • The specific areas or projects where my expertise is sought.
  • The proposed compensation.

I'm most likely to say yes when the role connects closely to my research: how AI affects the way people think and work, and designing AI as a tool for thought, and when the scope and commitments of the request are thoughtfully designed and commensurate with the proposed compensation structure.

Can you endorse me, or recommend someone...?

I get many requests to endorse arXiv submissions or to suggest alternative contacts, especially when I am unable to accept a speaking, advisory, or consultancy engagement myself. I can't do justice to these requests (and to my network) for people I don't know, so I'm afraid I can only make a connection or recommendation for you if we already have a working relationship. Therefore, I kindly ask that you only make such requests if we are already working together.
